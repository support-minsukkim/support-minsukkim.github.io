Mandra Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-23 (previous version: 2026-08-28)

한국어 · The Korean version prevails if the two differ.

Summary: Mandra has no accounts and no servers, and the developer collects no personal information. Your data is stored on your device, and if your operating system's backup (iCloud or Google backup) is turned on, it may be backed up to your own account.

App and developer

  • App: Mandra (iOS and Android, app ID com.mandraapp.app)
  • Developer: Minsuk Kim (individual)
  • Contact: support.minsukkim@gmail.com

Personal information we collect

None. Mandra has no sign-up or login and collects no personal information such as name, email, phone number or location. It contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or third-party tracking tools.

Data created and stored on your device

The data below is stored on your device for the app to work, and is never sent to the developer's servers or to third parties. (For operating-system backup, see "Operating-system backup" below.)

  • Mandalart data: goal, sub-goal and action text, color choice, created/updated times
  • Activity records: check-in dates per action (activity grid) and dates used to compute streaks
  • Profile display name (optional): used only for the in-app greeting
  • App settings: theme, language, notification times, lock-screen display (iOS), etc.
  • Home-screen widget and lock-screen (iOS Live Activity) display information: a summary of the data above and the page position chosen in the widget are shared with the widget only on your device

Operating-system backup

Mandra itself never sends your data off the device, but if your device's operating-system backup is turned on, app data (mandalarts, activity records, settings) may be backed up to your own account.

  • iOS: iCloud Backup of the device, or a computer backup
  • Android: Google account automatic backup (including device-to-device transfer). Information that only makes sense on one device, such as the widget page position and notification schedule state, is excluded from backup.

These backups are handled by Apple or Google as an operating-system feature, and the developer cannot access their contents. You can turn backups off or delete them in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Apple Account → iCloud; Android: Settings → System → Backup).

Diagnostics (on-device)

To improve stability, the iOS app keeps crash diagnostics provided by Apple's MetricKit on the device only. These diagnostics are never sent automatically; the only way they reach the developer is Apple's standard process when you agree to share with Apple in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). The Android app collects no diagnostics of its own (only the standard crash statistics Google Play provides when you have agreed to share them on Android).

Notifications

All notifications, such as check-in reminders and the weekly report, are local notifications scheduled on the device. No push server is used. Notification permission is optional and can be turned off at any time in your device settings.

Retention and deletion

  • Data on the device is deleted when you delete the app.
  • You can delete individual mandalarts in the app.
  • Copies left in an operating-system backup can be deleted in the device's backup settings.
  • There are no copies on the developer's servers, so no separate deletion request is needed.

Children's privacy

Mandra collects no personal information from anyone, and therefore none from children.

Planned features

If any of the following features is added, this policy will be updated before release and the change noted in the app's update notes.

  • In-app cloud sync (opt-in): only if you turn it on, mandalart data is synced to your own cloud storage (for example, iCloud's private database on iOS). The developer cannot access that data.
  • Pro subscription: payments are processed only by the Apple App Store or Google Play billing system; the developer does not collect card or other payment details.
  • AI features: if AI features such as goal breakdown are added, this policy will state what text is processed and where.

Changes to this policy

Changes are published on this page with the updated date. Significant changes are also noted in the app's update notes.
